# Decisions

Settled choices and the reasoning behind them, so they are not re-litigated. A decision is
reversible; the reasoning is what tells you whether reversing it is safe.

One decision per file, named by the anchor slug of its title, so a link names the decision it
cites and two branches settling two decisions touch two different files. A decision carries
cause, reason, decision and what it beat — the
[decision skill](https://github.com/effekt/nubbin/blob/main/.claude/skills/decision/SKILL.md)
walks the writing and the sweep that follows it.

These are cited from the pages whose behaviour they explain, which is how a reader meets one: a
decision answers a question a reference page raised. They are not in the sidebar, because
browsing forty of them is not how anyone arrives at the one they need. [The
directory](https://github.com/effekt/nubbin/tree/main/docs/decisions) lists every file for
anyone who does want to read them through.
